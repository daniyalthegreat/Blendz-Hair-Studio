Inside BLENDZ

A space built to feel deliberate.

The Tour

Four corners, one standard.

A walk through the studio — the stations, the details, and the moments that make BLENDZ feel like more than just a barbershop.

Station 01

The Mirror Wall

Deep sage walls meet warm ring lighting at every station — bright enough to see every line clean, soft enough that you're not sitting under a spotlight. Product shelves are kept minimal and visible, not hidden in drawers.

Station 02

The Styling Floor

The main floor runs under soft chandelier light rather than harsh overheads — a small choice that changes how the whole room feels, especially for facials and longer sessions.

Station 03

The Wash Chairs

Comfortable, reclined wash stations set beneath the same signature ring mirrors — part of the studio's rhythm rather than an afterthought tucked in the back.

Station 04

The Details

Framed calligraphy, curated product shelves, warm wood accents against the sage — the kind of detail most shops skip, and the kind that tells you someone actually cared about this space.
