A Few Things People Ask

Before your first visit.

Do I need to book in advance?

Walk-ins are welcome, but message us on WhatsApp first if you can — especially on Fridays and weekends when chairs fill up fast. It also lets us confirm pricing before you arrive.

How do I pay?

Cash and major mobile wallets (Easypaisa, JazzCash) are accepted in-studio. Payment is made after your service.

Can I request a specific stylist?

Yes — if you'd like Wasim specifically rather than a team member, mention it when you message us so we can plan the slot.

Is there parking nearby?

Street parking is available directly outside the studio in Block B, North Nazimabad Town.
