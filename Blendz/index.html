The Studio

Every chair here is earned, not filled.

Most barbershops in the city cut corners — untrained hands, no hygiene, zero eye for detail. BLENDZ was built as the opposite of that. Deep sage walls, brass fixtures, and ring-lit mirrors set the tone the moment you walk in, but it's the work in the chair that keeps people coming back.

Wasim brings almost a decade of experience to every fade, beard sculpt, and styling session, and it shows — in the 4.9 rating, and in clients who've followed him from his first job to his own studio.
